# Pitch Deck Outline

> Template. Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.

A 12-slide outline for a solo founder at pre-seed or seed. Copy it, fill each slide's fields, then cut. The playbook labels this order a synthesis (Inference), not any firm's template. Keep one idea per slide, and keep two versions: a short presented deck and a read-alone deck (same core, more text, plus an appendix).

Use it when you are building or rebuilding a deck for a first investor meeting. Fix cap table, IP assignment and founder-stock paperwork before the first meeting (see the data room checklist).

## Slide-by-slide

| # | Slide | Question it answers | Evidence to include | Fill in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title and one-liner | What is this, in one sentence? | Name, plain-language description, stage | `[Company]` does `[what]` for `[named customer]`; stage `[ ... ]` |
| 2 | Problem | Whose pain, how bad, how handled today? | Named customer, one concrete example, interview evidence | `[Who]` loses `[cost/time]` because `[cause]`; today they `[workaround]`; `[n]` interviews |
| 3 | Solution and product | What do you do, and does it work? | Screenshots or short demo, concrete benefit | `[Benefit]`; `[screenshot/demo link]` |
| 4 | Why now | What changed that makes this possible today? | One specific shift (technology, regulation, cost, behaviour) | `[Shift]` on `[date]` means `[effect on product]` |
| 5 | Traction | What proof exists? | Paying unaffiliated customers, MRR trend, retention, or signed LOIs/pilots | `[n]` paying customers (`[n]` strangers); MRR `[ ... ]`; each metric labelled actual / estimated / assumed |
| 6 | Business model and unit economics | How does it make money per customer? | Price, gross margin, CAC and payback, labelled actual or assumed | Price `[ ... ]`; gross margin `[ ... ]`; payback `[ ... ]` months |
| 7 | Market (bottom-up) | How big can this get, built from what? | Customers x price x frequency; top-down only as a cross-check | `[customers]` x `[price]` x `[frequency]` = `[ ... ]`; first segment `[ ... ]` |
| 8 | Competition and alternatives | Why you rather than the status quo or an incumbent? | Honest alternatives table, one durable edge | Alternatives `[ ... ]`; edge `[ ... ]` |
| 9 | Go-to-market | How will the next 100 customers be found? | One working channel with measured conversion | Channel `[ ... ]`; conversion `[ ... ]`; effort `[hours/spend]` |
| 10 | Team (solo) | Why you, and how is the single-founder risk covered? | Relevant track record, advisors or first hires, plan for gaps | Founder-market fit `[ ... ]`; gaps `[ ... ]`; hire triggers `[ ... ]` |
| 11 | Ask and milestones | How much, for how long, to reach what? | Amount, runway months, 2-4 measurable milestones, Series A-ready target | Raise `[amount]`; runway `[months]`; milestones `[1] [2] [3]` |
| 12 | Vision (optional) | What if it works? | One paragraph, not a forecast | `[ ... ]` |

Appendix: financial model, cohort tables, product detail, security/privacy summary, detailed competitor grid.

## Rules of thumb from the playbook

- Lead with problem, solution and why now in two or three slides; traction on slide 5; team on slide 10.
- Slides 1-5 should pass a two-minute skim (DocSend-reported review times were roughly 2:12 to 2:42 in 2021-2023; directional only, press-reported).
- Design norms (YC Demo Day guide): one main point per slide, about seven words or fewer, large fonts, high contrast, no complex graphs.
- Ask: YC advises leaving valuation and cap out of the deck; CRV puts the raise amount on the cover. Choose one deliberately.
- CRV's seed guide says founders typically target 18 to 24 months of runway (cash divided by monthly burn).

## Illustrative example (made-up numbers)

Slide 11, ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY: monthly burn 50,000 in your currency. 18 months of runway is 50,000 x 18 = 900,000; 24 months is 1,200,000 (the playbook uses the same arithmetic: $0.9M and $1.2M).

- Raise: `[900,000]` for 18 months of runway.
- Milestone 1: `[20]` paying customers, `[12]` of them unaffiliated.
- Milestone 2: monthly recurring revenue of `[ ... ]` with flat cohort retention.
- Milestone 3: one repeatable acquisition channel with measured conversion.

## Checklist before sending

- [ ] One-sentence description tested on three strangers.
- [ ] Core deck of 10-12 slides; appendix holds the rest.
- [ ] Traction labels actual vs assumed.
- [ ] Team slide answers "what if you get sick, stuck or overloaded?"
