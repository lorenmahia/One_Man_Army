# Weekly Business Review

> Template. Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.

A 30-45 minute weekly review for a one-person company, adapted in the playbook from public accounts of Amazon's weekly business review. Time boxes are starting points, not researched optima. Use it every week at a fixed calendar slot; one weekly review and one written monthly update carry most of the value.

Week of: `[YYYY-MM-DD]`   Time box: `[30-45 min]`

## Agenda

| Step | Question | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Customer | What did customers experience last week? | Support tickets, calls, churn reasons, 1-3 verbatim comments |
| 2. Scorecard | Which input and output metrics moved vs trailing 6 weeks and target? | 5-10 metrics max; flag only exceptions |
| 3. Why | For each exception: cause, or "unknown, follow-up scheduled"? | Do not invent explanations (applies to AI summaries too) |
| 4. OKR check | On track, at risk or off track per KR? | Notes only; no re-planning in the meeting |
| 5. Cash | Cash balance, 4-week burn, runway in months | One line |
| 6. Risks and decisions | Any risk-register item changed? Any decision to log? | Link to the decision log |
| 7. Next week | Top 3 commitments | Save where you will see them Monday |

## Scorecard (5-10 metrics)

Inputs are controllable (outreach sent, demos booked); outputs lag (revenue). Prefer inputs for steering. Write each definition once, one system of record per number (payments processor for revenue, bank for cash, product analytics for usage), and record the date on every number.

## Time budget

Estimate (illustrative): about 2.5 hours per week plus 3 hours per month plus 1 day per quarter is roughly 5-8% of a 40-50 hour week. If admin exceeds about 10% of working time, cut metrics before cutting customer contact.
