# Investor Target List

> Template. Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.

A scored target list of 30-60 investors, built only from public sources, plus a pipeline tracker. It turns "pitch everyone" into deliberate bets. Use it before outreach, set a 4-6 week window, and review the pipeline every Friday. Every score needs a public URL as evidence. Do not buy scraped personal contact lists, guess private email addresses or harvest personal data; use firm contact forms and public professional channels.

## 1. Research columns (one row per investor)

| Column | What to record |
|---|---|
| Investor / firm | `[name]` (public name only) |
| Thesis | Sectors, models, customers, anti-thesis ("we do not invest in...") |
| Stage and role | Pre-seed / seed; lead or follow; do they take first cheques? |
| Cheque size | From the firm site, or inferred from fund size and portfolio count (mark as Estimate) |
| Geography | Do they back companies outside their home region, under what entity? |
| Portfolio conflicts | Direct competitors in the last 3-5 years, adjacent bets, exits |
| Recent activity | Announcements in the last 6-12 months; fund recently closed or deploying its last fund? |
| Founder signals | What portfolio founders say publicly; the investor's own published guides |
| Source URL | `[public URL for each score]` |

## 2. Scoring rubric

Score each criterion 0-3 (0 = no or unknown, 3 = strong public evidence), then multiply by the weight.

| Criterion | Weight | 0 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thesis fit | x3 | No sector overlap | Explicit thesis names your category |
| Stage fit | x2 | Series A+ only | Invests first cheques at your stage |
| Cheque fit | x2 | Cheque far above/below need | Cheque covers 20-50% of your round |
| Geography / entity fit | x2 | Will not back your jurisdiction | Has backed companies in your country |
| Conflict risk | x2 (inverted) | Direct competitor in portfolio | No conflicts |
| Recent activity | x1 | No deals in 12 months | 2+ relevant deals in 6 months |
| Access (warm path) | x2 | No path | Warm intro available |

- Max score: (3+2+2+2+2+1+2) x 3 = 42.
- Tier A: 32 or more. Tier B: 24-31. Tier C: below 24.
- Mix for 30-60 names: about 10-15 Tier A, 15-25 Tier B, the rest Tier C or angels who write small cheques. The weights are a starting point; adjust after the first five conversations.
- Contact Tier B first to practise, then Tier A in a compressed window.

## 3. Pipeline tracker

Columns: `Investor | Tier | Score | Source URL | Warm path | First contact | Status | Last touch | Next step + date | Cheque indicated | Notes/conflicts | Decision`

Statuses: Not contacted, Sent, Replied, Meeting booked, Met, Diligence, Term sheet/SAFE, Passed, Closed.

Follow-up cadence (playbook Inference): day 0 send; day 5-7 one short follow-up with new progress; day 14-21 a final note with a milestone; then move to the monthly update list. After a meeting: thank-you within 24 hours, answers within 48.
