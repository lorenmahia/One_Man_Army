# Risk Register

> Template. Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.

A five-to-ten row risk register for a one-person company. The playbook identifies key-person risk and sunk-cost drift as a solo founder's extra risks; a register plus pre-agreed kill criteria addresses both cheaply. Review the top five monthly and the whole list at the quarterly reset. Owner is always you; naming a backup is part of the mitigation.

## How to use

- Score likelihood and impact 1-5; Score = likelihood x impact.
- Early warning: the observable signal that the risk is materialising.
- Kill criteria: the pre-agreed threshold at which you stop or change course. Write them while calm, sign and date them, and move them only through a logged decision (see the decision log). Practitioner sources warn that teams lower the bar or extend deadlines as a threshold approaches.
- Run one 20-minute pre-mortem before each quarter's biggest bet (assume it failed, list reasons) and add the top three causes here.

## Key-person risk row and continuity pack

Minimum viable continuity pack (playbook Inference): one page listing accounts, domains, payment processors, bank, legal entity documents, customers to notify, a trusted person with emergency access, and a documented "pause and wind-down" procedure. Store credentials in a password manager with emergency access; review quarterly. Consider key-person insurance and a succession note, and check legal and insurance details with a professional.

## Runway thresholds and gate (illustrative)

Model base, downside (revenue minus 30%, costs flat) and upside cases monthly. Thresholds in the playbook are illustrative, not researched: at 9 months of runway start cost cuts; at 6 months run the stop/adjust review.

| Gate | Evidence required | Go | Adjust | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem validated | 15-20 interviews; 3 pre-orders | Proceed to build | Narrow segment or message | Stop if no one will pay |
| Product pull | Activation and week-4 retention targets | Invest in growth | Fix onboarding/scope | Stop if retention near zero after 2 iterations |
| Efficiency | CAC payback and runway | Scale spend | Cut cost, change channel | Stop or sell if runway is under `[N]` months with no path |
