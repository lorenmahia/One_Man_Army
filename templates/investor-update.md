# Investor Update

> Template. Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.

A short monthly update with a normal version and a bad-month variant. The playbook's outline is a synthesis (Inference) of YC-attributed advice (secondhand) and other public templates: lead with metrics, put specific asks near the top, end with a short recap. Send it even with no investors (to advisors or yourself); it forces a monthly close and an honest read of the numbers. Do the close by about working day 5 so data is fresh. Keep it short and consistent; it is a habit.

3. Asks (specific and named; max three): `[intro to ...]`, `[advice on ...]`, `[hiring help ...]`
4. Highlights (wins) and lowlights (misses), each with one line on cause: `[ ... ]`
5. Priorities for next month, tied to OKRs: `[ ... ]`
6. Recap (2-3 lines): `[ ... ]`
7. Appendix only if needed.

## B. Bad-month variant

The playbook cites Founder Collective advice (2018): disclose early, own mistakes, phone serious news rather than writing it, and mix good and bad news in regular updates. It cites SaaStr on confidence-killers: surprises, unclear runway, rosy updates, blaming the market. Inference: lead with the miss and the number.

Subject: `[Company] update - [Month YYYY] - RED: [the miss in a few words]`

1. The miss, with the number: `[Metric] was [x] against a plan of [y].`
2. Cause: what we know `[ ... ]`; what we do not yet know `[ ... ]`.
3. Runway: new cash-out date `[date]`; runway `[months]` in the base case and `[months]` in the downside case.
4. Plan: actions `[1] [2] [3]`; decision date `[date]`; the kill/adjust threshold we are watching `[ ... ]`.
5. One specific ask: `[ ... ]`
6. What is still working (brief, no spin): `[ ... ]`

Before sending a serious miss, call the lead investor or advisor one-on-one first.

## Example (ILLUSTRATIVE, made-up numbers)

Subject: Acme update - March - RED: MRR down, runway now 9 months

"MRR fell from 4,000 to 3,400 after two customers cancelled. Both cited the same missing integration; we do not yet know whether others feel the same, and we are interviewing five more customers this week. Cash-out date is now December in the base case (9 months) and October in a downside case with revenue 30% lower. Plan: ship the integration by 15 April, decision on whether to continue investing on 30 April. Ask: one intro to a customer using a competing tool."
