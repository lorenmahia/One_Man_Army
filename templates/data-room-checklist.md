# Data Room Checklist

> Template. Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.

A folder structure and stage-by-stage checklist for investor diligence. Prepare early, share late: early meetings need only the deck and a summary; the full room opens at investment-committee review or term-sheet stage. Copy the table, set the Status column, and date-stamp versions. The stage split is the playbook's Inference; sources disagree on exact items, so have a startup lawyer confirm jurisdiction-specific items.

## Folder structure

```
00 Executive Summary
01 Financials
02 Legal
03 Go-to-Market
04 Product
05 HR
06 Prior financings
07 Regulatory and privacy
```

Folders 00-05 follow CRV's suggestion; 06 and 07 are the playbook's additions. Never leave drafts beside executed copies.

## Access layers

1. View-only deck link with an expiry.
2. Summary and headline financials.
3. Core corporate and financial documents after NDA or serious interest.
4. Sensitive contracts and cohort data for the lead investor only.

Use a virtual data room (or permissioned folder) with audit logs and per-investor links, not public file links. Refresh monthly; revoke access after the round closes.

## Red flags to clear before the first meeting

- [ ] Cap table ties to signed SAFEs, notes, grants and approvals; share counts match across cap table, model and legal ledger.
- [ ] Founder stock properly issued; 83(b) election filed if applicable (30-day deadline; US-specific).
- [ ] IP assignments from founders, employees and contractors, including outside dev teams.
- [ ] No informal advisor/contractor arrangements or verbal equity promises.
- [ ] Deck figures (e.g. ARR) match accounting files; no "final FINAL" versions.
- [ ] No public or unrestricted links to sensitive documents.
