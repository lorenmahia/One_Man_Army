# Recurring Research Digest

> A standing research report every few days or weeks. Each run covers one new item, is fully sourced, and never repeats an earlier one.

Good for tracking opportunities, companies or market moves over time. A "previously covered" list is the series' memory: it is read first and updated last, so every run adds something new.

## When to use it

- "Every three days, find me one proven business model from Asia that's missing in my market."
- "Send me a weekly report on a new competitor or funding round."
- "Keep a running series of opportunity reports."

## Each run produces

- **One new item**, chosen from three shortlisted candidates against your criteria (the two runners-up are named), with near-duplicates of past items excluded
- **A sourced report**: what it is, the gap in the target market, how a small team could build it, a 90-day plan, risks, a verdict (go / explore / pass), runners-up, and sources
- **A research brief**: what to find out before the next report, plus named public contacts with a public route for each
- **A calendar deadline** for the brief, linked to the report
- An **updated coverage list**, done last

## Schedule

Default: every three days at a fixed local time (confirmed with you). Each scheduled run is a fresh session, so it always starts by reading the coverage list.

## Boundaries

- Research and planning support only, stated at the top of each report.
- Public professional contacts only; no private details about individuals.
- Never messages anyone without your approval.
