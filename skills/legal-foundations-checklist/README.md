# Legal Foundations Checklist

> A status checklist for founder equity, IP, trademarks, first contracts and worker classification, plus 15 questions to bring to a startup lawyer.

**Not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.** It never drafts final contract text or says a template is valid in your jurisdiction; confirm with a qualified lawyer.

## When to use it

- "What legal paperwork does my startup need in its first month?"
- "Should I vest my own shares as a solo founder, and what is an 83(b) election?"
- "I used a freelancer for my code. Do I own it?"
- "Which contracts come first, and what should I never concede?"

## What you get

- A status table (done / not started / unclear) across equity, IP, trademarks, contracts and hiring
- Founder equity and vesting points to discuss, with the US 83(b) deadline flagged
- An IP review: founder assignment, contractor assignments, open-source licences, AI-assisted work
- Contracts in priority order, with the clauses to understand in each
- Worker-classification red flags and the official test to run
- The 15 questions for a startup lawyer, tailored to your answers

## How it works

1. Loads the playbook (from the repo or from GitHub).
2. Asks one round of intake questions: country, stage, co-founders, who built what, name and data handling.
3. Walks equity, IP, trademarks, contracts and hiring, quoting dated figures as snapshots.
4. Delivers the status table, next three actions and the lawyer question list.

## Boundaries

- Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.
- No contract text is drafted as final; templates are starting points only.
- It never signs, files or submits anything for you.
- Fees and legal positions change; unverified items are marked and must be re-checked.
