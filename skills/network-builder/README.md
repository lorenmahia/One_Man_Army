# Network Builder

> Build a network from zero with a stakeholder map, a public-information target list, vetted communities, careful outreach, advisor and partnership structures, and a light contact tracker.

## When to use it

- "I'm new to this industry and know nobody. Where do I start?"
- "Which communities should I join, and how do I tell good ones from bad?"
- "How much equity should I offer an advisor?"
- "How do I set up a small pilot with a potential partner?"

## What you get

- A stakeholder map by need: customers, advisors, talent, investors, partners, press.
- A target list built from public professional information only.
- A community shortlist with good signals and red flags to check.
- An outreach cadence, with message drafting handed to another skill.
- Advisor equity ranges with dates and sources, and a partnership pilot outline.
- A contact-tracker field list and a Week 1 / Month 1 / Quarter 1 plan.

## How it works

It reads the playbook, asks one round of questions (venture, country, top need, existing contacts, time), builds the map and lists, then sets the cadence, advisor and pilot structures. Numbers appear as dated ranges with their source type.

## Boundaries

- Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.
- Public professional information only; it does not collect private data.
- It never sends messages, posts or contacts anyone for you, and it respects anti-spam law and platform rules.
- Equity and partner terms are norms, not offers; have a lawyer review agreements.
