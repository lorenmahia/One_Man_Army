# Fundraising Round Planner

> Compare SAFEs, notes and priced rounds, model your own dilution, plan the timeline, and prepare questions for your lawyer.

## When to use it

- "SAFE or convertible note for my first round?"
- "How much of the company will I own after my seed round?"
- "What does an option pool top-up do to my ownership?"
- "How long does a raise take and what should I ask a lawyer?"

## What you get

- An instrument comparison with pros, cons and typical use.
- A step-by-step cap-table model using your numbers, with formulae shown and arithmetic rechecked (labelled illustrative).
- Benchmark ranges for dilution, valuation and round size, with dates.
- A process timeline and legal-cost ranges.
- A list of questions for your lawyer and accountant.

## How it works

Loads the playbook, asks one round of intake questions, compares instruments, runs the dilution formulae, places results against dated ranges, builds the timeline, and writes the professional-advice question list.

## Boundaries

Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice. It never recommends a specific deal, valuation or investor, and does not draft or sign legal documents. Benchmarks are snapshots and many items are US-centric.
