# Founder Outreach Drafts

> Short outreach messages in your own voice, with one clear ask each, for networking, partner, advisor and customer introductions.

Short messages get replies. Every draft says who you are, why this person, and one specific ask that is easy to say yes to, in under 90 words.

## When to use it

- "Draft a connect note to the head of the fintech association."
- "Write a cold email asking this founder for a 15-minute call."
- "I need an intro message to a potential distributor."

## What you get

- A **three-part message**: who you are (one line) → why them (one specific reason from their public work) → one ask with a time window
- **Two variants**: one for peers, one for senior people (regulators and officials get a formal route, not a cold message)
- A **follow-up date** 5–7 days later, with a short second line

## Voice and venture safety

- Uses your saved tone or opener if you have one.
- Checks which venture the message is for, and **never carries over** names, websites or products from a different project. If a saved opener doesn't fit, it writes a neutral one and tells you.

## Boundaries

- **Drafts only.** It never sends on your behalf without explicit approval.
- Uses only public professional information about the recipient. It never guesses email addresses.
- Leaves out personal details you haven't chosen to share.
