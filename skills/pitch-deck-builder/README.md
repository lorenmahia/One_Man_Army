# Pitch Deck Builder

> Plan an evidence-backed pitch deck, rehearse the hard questions, and prepare a data room for your stage.

## When to use it

- "Help me structure my seed pitch deck."
- "What will investors ask me, and how should I answer as a solo founder?"
- "What goes in a data room at pre-seed?"
- "Turn my notes into a deck outline."

## What you get

- A 12-slide recommended order with the question each slide answers.
- A per-slide evidence checklist (what you have, what is missing, common mistakes).
- Answer skeletons for the 20 most common investor questions.
- A data-room folder structure and checklist for pre-seed, seed or Series A.
- An outline or draft in whatever deck tooling you have.

## How it works

Loads the playbook, asks one round of intake questions, proposes the slide order, checks evidence and the ask, prepares Q&A, lists data-room items, and offers to build the deck.

## Boundaries

Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice. It will not invent traction or numbers, and never sends, uploads or signs anything for you. Deck-reading statistics are older snapshots and treated as directional.
