# SEO and GEO Launch Plan

> Build a technical-SEO checklist, a narrow content plan, a GEO/AEO plan that separates evidence from speculation, and a 90-day schedule for a new site.

## When to use it

- "I just launched a site with no traffic. How do I get found on Google and in ChatGPT?"
- "Make me an SEO and GEO plan for my SaaS, English and Spanish."
- "Is llms.txt worth doing? What actually helps AI answers cite me?"
- "Set up how I measure whether AI engines mention my brand."

## What you get

- A technical-SEO pass/fail checklist for your stack.
- One narrow topic cluster mapped to search intents.
- A GEO/AEO table labelled Fact, Inference and Vendor hype to discount.
- A baseline and monthly prompt-test sheet.
- A 90-day plan sized to your weekly hours.

## How it works

It reads the playbook, asks one round of questions (site, market, language, competitors, time), then works through technical setup, content, GEO, measurement and the schedule. Every action cites a playbook section and is marked evidenced or speculative.

## Boundaries

- Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.
- Figures are dated snapshots from a fast-moving area; re-check before relying on them.
- It never posts, submits or contacts anyone for you, and it does not suggest paid links or fake mentions.
- No ranking, citation or traffic promises.
