# Hiring and Scaling Planner

> Decide between hiring, a contractor or automation, cost it with your own numbers, and scale one gated step at a time.

## When to use it

- "Should I hire someone or use a freelancer?"
- "What would a first employee really cost me?"
- "Which tasks can I safely hand to AI agents?"
- "I want to expand to another country. What should I check first?"

## What you get

- **A hire / contractor / agency / automate recommendation** for your bottleneck
- **A fully loaded cost calculation** using your numbers and your runway
- **A structured-interview and work-sample plan** with a role scorecard
- **A 30-60-90 onboarding plan**
- **A systems-before-headcount checklist**
- **AI-agent delegation boundaries** with human-in-the-loop controls
- **A new-market expansion gate** with go / adjust / stop criteria

## How it works

```
Intake questions -> Hire / contractor / automate decision -> Loaded cost
   -> Interview + work sample -> 30-60-90 -> Systems checklist
   -> AI boundaries -> Market gate
```

## Boundaries

- Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.
- Employment law varies by country; it points to official sources and a local professional.
- Benchmarks are mostly US or large-company snapshots, treated as ranges.
- It never contacts candidates, posts jobs or makes offers.
