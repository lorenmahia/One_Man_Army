# Go-to-Market Planner

> Choose a beachhead, position and price your product, pick one channel, plan your first 10 customers and set a go / adjust / stop gate before you launch.

## When to use it

- "Who should I sell to first, and how do I reach them?"
- "Help me position my product against what people use today."
- "Which channel fits a local service business with no budget?"
- "I'm launching next month. What should I have ready, and how will I know it's working?"

## What you get

- A one-line ICP and a beachhead choice with scored candidates.
- A one-page positioning canvas and tests to validate it.
- A pricing approach, with paid-pilot terms and a price-testing plan.
- A channel shortlist for your business type, plus a first-10-customers plan.
- A launch checklist and a go / adjust / stop gate with criteria written in advance.

## How it works

It reads the playbook, asks one round of questions about your product, buyers and constraints, then builds the plan section by section. Benchmarks appear as dated ranges and every item cites a playbook section.

## Boundaries

- Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.
- Benchmarks are noisy, vendor-biased snapshots; re-check before relying on them.
- It never sends outreach, posts launches or contacts anyone for you.
- Gate thresholds in the playbook are placeholders; you set your own.
