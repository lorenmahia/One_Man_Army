# Investor Research Pipeline

> Decide whether venture funding fits, then build a scored list of 30-60 investors from public sources, with a tracker and outreach plan.

## When to use it

- "Should I raise venture money or bootstrap?"
- "Help me build a target list of seed investors for my startup."
- "How do I organise my fundraising pipeline and outreach?"
- "Which funding sources fit a solo founder outside the US?"

## What you get

- A fit-test verdict (venture vs revenue-first) with reasons.
- A scored target list (tiers A/B/C) with a public source URL beside every score.
- Tracker columns and statuses you can paste into a sheet.
- A 4-6 week batching plan and weekly review prompt.

## How it works

Loads the playbook, asks one round of intake questions, runs the fit test, checks entity gates, researches public sources only, scores each investor with the playbook template, then hands off to outreach drafting.

## Boundaries

Research and planning support only; not legal, tax, investment or financial advice. Uses public professional information only, never guesses private contacts, and never sends messages for you. Playbook figures are dated snapshots; unverified items are marked.
