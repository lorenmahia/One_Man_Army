# Company Setup Navigator

> A guided intake that shortlists where and what to incorporate, names the official registry and tax authority, and covers banking, tax triggers and finance basics.

**Not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.** Fees and thresholds are dated snapshots; confirm everything with the official body and a qualified local professional.

## When to use it

- "I live in Kenya and want US investors. Where should I incorporate?"
- "Which registry do I use for a UK limited company, and what should I confirm there?"
- "When do I have to register for VAT or sales tax if I sell software abroad?"
- "What documents will a bank ask for, and why do accounts get frozen?"

## What you get

- A ranked shortlist of entity and jurisdiction options with the reasons and risks
- The official registry and tax authority for each shortlisted country, with fees quoted by date
- Banking and payments options, and the documents usually requested
- Tax-registration triggers mapped to your sales, with thresholds as dated examples
- A finance-operations setup and a Week 1 / Month 1 / Quarter 1 plan
- Question lists for a lawyer and an accountant

## How it works

1. Loads the playbook (from the repo or from GitHub).
2. Asks one round of intake questions: residence, market, investors, budget, regulation, banking needs.
3. Applies the playbook's decision method and table, then names the official bodies.
4. Maps banking, tax triggers and finance basics, and delivers one document with open questions marked.

## Boundaries

- Research and planning support only; it does not give legal, tax, investment or financial advice.
- It never files, registers, signs or applies for anything.
- It does not invent fees, thresholds or registry names; unverified items are marked.
- Countries outside the playbook's examples need their registry looked up and confirmed.
